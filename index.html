I was having my morning tea and thinking about our chat—specifically when you called me a puzzle with too many hidden sections, while describing yourself as simple and uninteresting.

It made me laugh, but it also made me realize something. You’ve got it backwards. You aren't simple at all...

You’re simply only for those who give a quick look, but for who looks closely, you’re sophisticated at least.

When you look close enough, you see vibrant colors beautifully merging streaks that should be opposites:

Caring & warm yet sexy & seductive
Softie & sensitive yet resilient & reliable
Frank & blunt yet unselfish

Together they form blue, a beautiful color, but never simple or to be taken for granted.

No wonder it's also your favorite color.

Happy Birthday, Beccy!

I wish you a long, happy life. I might even envy you because only a few whom time plays in the favor of their beauty.

I'm glad I opened chitchat that night of March 6th, and I look forward to discovering more shades and layers of that color that is you.

— Saiff
