The Canvas I · V
A thought, over morning tea

I was thinking about our chat the other day — specifically you called me a puzzle with too many hidden sections, while saying that you're simple and not that interesting.

It made me laugh because you've got it slightly wrong.

The surface

Only for those who look on the surface, you're simple...

...But for who look closely, you're sophisticated and interesting.

Fiercely warm & caring
yet sexy & seductive
A total softie & sensitive
yet resilient & reliable
Blunt & frank
yet deeply unselfish
Private & guarded
yet generous with those you trust
The blend

Together they form blue — a beautiful color, but never simple or to be taken for granted.

No wonder it's also your favorite color.

The toast

Happy Birthday,
Beccy.

I wish you a long, happy life. I might even envy you — because only a few, time plays in the favor of their beauty.

I'm glad I opened Chitchat that night of March 6th. I look forward to discovering more shades and layers of that color that is you.

— Saiff
